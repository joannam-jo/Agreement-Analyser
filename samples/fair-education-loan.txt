EDUCATION LOAN AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Bharat National Bank (the Lender) and the Borrower and Co-Borrower named in the Schedule.

1. Loan Amount
The Lender shall disburse a loan of Rs. 6,00,000 directly to the institution in instalments as per the fee schedule. Repayment shall be made in 84 equated monthly instalments.

2. Interest Rate
Interest shall be charged at a floating rate linked to the Lender's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR), currently 9.5% per annum. The rate resets every three months when the benchmark changes. The Borrower will be informed in writing before any reset takes effect.

3. Interest During Course and Moratorium
Simple interest shall accrue during the course period and the moratorium period of one year after course completion. Interest not paid during this period will be capitalised and added to the principal when repayment begins. The Borrower may pay the interest during the course to avoid capitalisation.

4. Processing Fee
A processing fee of 1% of the loan amount plus GST is payable, as set out in the Key Fact Statement.

5. Prepayment
The Borrower may prepay the loan in full or in part at any time. No prepayment charges or foreclosure charges shall apply.

6. Co-Borrower
The parent named as Co-Borrower shall be jointly and severally liable for repayment of the loan.

7. Late Payment
Penal charges of 2% per annum on the overdue amount shall apply for the period of default, as disclosed in the Key Fact Statement.

8. Insurance
Life insurance cover for the Borrower is optional. The Borrower may choose any insurer.

9. Set-off
The Lender may exercise a right of set-off against deposits held by the Borrower with the Lender after giving 15 days' written notice.

10. Grievance Redressal
Complaints may be raised with the Lender's Grievance Redressal Officer. If not resolved within 30 days, the Borrower may approach the RBI Ombudsman.

Signed by the Borrower, Co-Borrower and the Lender.
