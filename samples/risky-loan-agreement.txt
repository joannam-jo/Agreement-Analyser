LOAN AGREEMENT - SAMPLE FOR TESTING

Section 1. Interest Rate. The lender may adjust the variable interest rate at its sole discretion without notice to the borrower. Your monthly EMI may increase substantially.

Section 2. Auto-Renewal. This agreement shall auto-renew for successive twelve-month periods unless cancelled through a process that may be difficult to cancel. Cancellation requests may be subject to a processing fee not disclosed at signup.

Section 3. Penalties. Late payments incur penalty fees of 5% per month. All fees are non-refundable. Liquidated damages apply if you terminate early.

Section 4. Arbitration. You waive your right to a jury trial and agree to binding arbitration in the lender's jurisdiction.

Section 5. Transparency. You may cancel anytime with 30-day written notice. No hidden fees apply to the principal balance disclosed in Schedule A.
